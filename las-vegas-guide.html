The Main Event · Six People, Seven Nights

The Week

This is the single best week of the year to bring a group to Las Vegas. The Formula 1 circus packs out Nov 19 to 21 and then the whole city exhales. You land into the quietest, cheapest, most available stretch on the calendar, with every show still running and every restaurant desperate for a table of six.

Timing · Read This First

You just missed the race by one day

The 2026 Formula 1 Heineken Las Vegas Grand Prix runs Thursday Nov 19 through Saturday Nov 21, with the race itself at 8pm Saturday night on the Strip circuit. Your trip opens Sunday Nov 22. If the group wants the race, move the front of the trip up two days and arrive Thursday the 19th. If not, you inherit the best possible version of the aftermath: grandstands coming down, the city half empty, and Sunday night rates a fraction of Saturday.

Decision to make now. Everything else in this guide works either way.
The Anchor Nights

Friday Nov 27 is the loaded night

Three headline options land on the same evening: the Eagles at Sphere, Journey at MGM Grand Garden Arena, and Lisa opening the back half of her Viva La Lisa residency at the Colosseum. Saturday Nov 28 repeats Eagles and Lisa and adds the Jonas Brothers. Pick your night early and buy six seats together before the weekend inventory thins.

Six together at Sphere is the hardest ask. Buy first, plan dinner around it.
Thanksgiving

Thursday Nov 26 needs a reservation, not a plan

Vegas does Thanksgiving beautifully and the good rooms sell out in October when the menus publish. A party of six on Thanksgiving is exactly the size that gets squeezed. Lock a table the week the menus drop, and hold a backup.

Full Thanksgiving section below with the rooms worth booking.

Safety and practicalities

  • The Strip and Summerlin are safe and heavily policed. Fremont Street after midnight is the one place to keep your group tight and your phone in a pocket.
  • The desert is dry beyond what your body signals. Drink well past thirst, especially on run days and drinking days.
  • Distances on the Strip are deceptive. What looks like two blocks is a 25 minute walk through three casinos.
  • Buy show tickets through the venue or Ticketmaster, never from a person on the sidewalk.
  • Nevada allows cannabis for adults over 21 but consumption is illegal in public, in casinos and in hotel rooms. Licensed lounges only.
  • Book Red Rock timed entry the moment your 30 day window opens. It sells out on holiday weekends.

Section 3

The Race Car Day

The one thing this city does that nowhere else does at this scale. Three genuinely different experiences, all bookable for six.

Formula 1 Heineken Las Vegas Grand Prix · Nov 19 to 21, 2026

Practice one and two on Thursday Nov 19 at 4:30pm and 8pm. Practice three and qualifying on Friday Nov 20 at 4:30pm and 8pm. The race itself at 8pm on Saturday Nov 21, run at night on a 3.8 mile circuit straight down Las Vegas Boulevard past the Sphere and the Bellagio fountains. It finishes the night before your trip opens. If the group wants it, shift arrival to Thursday the 19th and treat the race as the front end of the week. If not, you arrive to a city with the grandstands still up, the paddock breaking down, and hotel rates roughly a third of what they were 48 hours earlier.

Exotics Racing at SPEEDVEGAS · the pick for six

Twenty minutes south of the Strip at 14200 South Las Vegas Boulevard. A fleet of around 50 exotics and 23 race cars on a private 1.5 mile road course with real corners, not an oval. A professional coach rides with you and talks you through every lap. Minimum five laps, and the group can each take a different car and then swap. Ferrari 488, Lamborghini Huracan, McLaren, Porsche GT3. Book the morning slot for the best track temperature and the emptiest paddock, and add the drift or ride along package for anyone who does not want to drive.

Format
Road course, coach in car
Minimum
Five laps per car
Group of six
Easily accommodated, book together

20 min from the Strip

NASCAR at Las Vegas Motor Speedway · the Richard Petty style experience

Completely different feel. A stock car, a 1.5 mile banked superspeedway, and eight laps of flat out driving with a spotter in your ear instead of a coach in the seat. The rookie experience is the standard entry and there are ride along options for anyone who would rather be a passenger at 160 miles an hour. Twenty five minutes north of the Strip. Note that the Petty branded program has moved operators in recent years, so confirm which company is running the LVMS program when you book and buy direct.

How to build the day

Exotics in the morning, lunch somewhere unhurried, and then leave the afternoon open. Do not stack the speedway and the road course on the same day, the adrenaline flattens out and the second one wastes money. If the group splits, half can do Topgolf while the drivers run laps, then meet for a late lunch at the Wynn.

Section 7

Running

Late November in the Mojave is close to perfect running weather. Cool mornings, no humidity, and the best desert singletrack within twenty minutes of a Strip hotel.

The long run: Red Rock, Saturday Nov 28 at first light

Start at the Calico Basin or Red Spring lot before 8am and you skip the timed entry requirement entirely, since a vehicle through the fee station before 8am or after 5pm does not need a reservation. From Calico Basin run the Calico Hills and Kraft Mountain loop system, roughly a ten mile circuit on red sandstone and hardpack, then add the Grand Circle Loop for eleven more with about 1,100 feet of gain. That combination gives you a clean twenty mile day with real vertical and views that make the whole trip worth it.

Two warnings. The sand sections are far more expensive than the pace suggests, so run by effort and not by watch. And the desert takes water out of you without telling you, so carry more than a coastal twenty would need, plus electrolytes.

The alternative long run: Blue Diamond and Cottonwood

If Red Rock is busy on the holiday weekend, the Blue Diamond and Cottonwood trail network just south is the better twenty miler anyway. More singletrack, fewer people, more shade, and the little village of Blue Diamond at the trailhead for coffee afterward.

Daily easy miles from the Strip

The Strip itself is a bad run, too many crossings and too much crowd. Instead take a five minute ride to the Las Vegas Wash Trail or run the loop inside the Wynn and Encore golf perimeter. The single best easy run in the city is the Historic Railroad Trail out of Boulder City, seven miles of flat crushed gravel through five lit tunnels with Lake Mead on your right. Do that one at sunrise on Thanksgiving morning before the table.

For the cook at home nights and for stocking six people with real food between the big meals. Everything here delivers or is a short ride from the Strip.

Whole Foods

Whole Foods Market

Town Square · South Strip

Ten minutes south of Mandalay Bay and the closest full Whole Foods to the Strip. Good prepared food counter for a group lunch. Also at Summerlin and Henderson.

Premium

Sprouts Farmers Market

Multiple · closest at Flamingo

Strong produce and bulk sections at better prices than Whole Foods. The most convenient real grocery to a central Strip hotel.

Gourmet

Total Wine and the Wynn Marketplace

Strip and Off Strip

For a room stocked properly. Total Wine on Sahara has the deepest list in the state at retail rather than resort pricing.

Asian Market

99 Ranch Market

Chinatown · Spring Mountain Rd

Enormous, excellent seafood counter, and the best produce prices in the city. Worth a trip even just to walk it.

Farmers Market

Downtown Summerlin Farmers Market

Summerlin · Saturday mornings

The best of the valley markets, and directly on the way back from a Red Rock run. Local produce, bread and coffee.

Delivery

Instacart and Amazon Fresh

Citywide

Both cover the Strip hotels and will deliver to a bell desk. The correct move on arrival day rather than losing an hour to a store run.
