The Main Event · One Year

The Anniversary

This is the reason for the trip. Lima happens to be the best dining city on the continent, so the celebration writes itself. Book the marquee tables the day you lock flights, then build the rest of the day around one unforgettable dinner.

A note on timing: Lima is hosting The World's 50 Best Restaurants ceremony in November 2026, so the whole city is running at peak culinary energy this year. Tables at the top rooms are tighter than usual. Reserve early and mention the anniversary when you book. Most of these kitchens will do something special if they know.

The Splurge Table

Central

Virgilio Martinez and Pia Leon's ecosystem tasting menu, plated by altitude from the Pacific floor to the high Andes. A former World's Best Restaurant and a genuine bucket list meal. Around 1,600 soles per person before pairings.

Book via Resy weeks ahead. Ask for the chef's counter and flag the anniversary.
World's Best 2025

Maido

Mitsuharu Tsumura's Nikkei temple, named the World's Best Restaurant in 2025. The 50 hour short rib and the tiraditos are the signatures. The Experience menu runs around 1,295 soles per person.

Reserve at maido.pe. Bookings open 60 days out and vanish fast. Set a reminder for 30 days prior.
Quietly Spectacular

Kjolle

Pia Leon's own room inside the Central complex, ingredient led and seasonal, ranked among the very top restaurants worldwide. A softer, more intimate energy than the two giants and easier to land.

A strong plan B for the big night, or a second marquee lunch.
Sunset & Ocean

Cliffside in Miraflores

For a romantic view meal, aim for golden hour over the Pacific. Rosa Nautica sits on its own pier over the waves, and Larcomar's terraces put the ocean at your feet. Time the reservation to sunset and walk the Malecon after.

Not the food peak of the city, but the view and the moment carry it.
Dinner Among Ruins

Huaca Pucllana

Contemporary Peruvian dining beside a floodlit pre Incan pyramid in the middle of Miraflores. Genuinely theatrical and one of the most memorable settings in the city for a milestone.

Request a terrace table facing the huaca.
Private & Hands On

A Splurge Experience for Two

Layer one signature experience into the trip: paragliding off the Miraflores cliffs at sunset, a private pisco and Nikkei tasting tour, a couples spa afternoon, or a private cooking class with a market run and pisco sour lesson.

Paragliding launches right at Parque del Amor. Book the tandem sunset slot.
Barranco · Relais & Chateaux

Hotel B

A restored Belle Epoque mansion turned art hotel in bohemian Barranco. Intimate, characterful, and walkable to the district's galleries and bars. The most romantic small hotel in the city.

Miraflores · Belmond

Miraflores Park, A Belmond Hotel

All suite, clifftop, rooftop pool over the Pacific. Play the Signature or Limena Suite for a private terrace, plunge pool, and ocean views. The Belo Bar pours one of the best pisco sours in Lima.

San Isidro · Grande Dame

Country Club Lima Hotel

Old world glamour, colonial architecture, museum grade Peruvian art on the walls. Quieter and more classic. Book a Presidential Suite if you want the grand gesture.

Safety, tuned to your districts

  • Miraflores, Barranco and San Isidro are the safe, walkable bases. Treat it like any big city, aware but relaxed.
  • Move point to point by Uber or Cabify after dark rather than walking long stretches.
  • Keep your phone off the table at sidewalk cafes and out of sight near traffic. Snatch and grab is the main risk.
  • From the airport, book Uber from the marked pickup zone or an authorized counter. Budget 45 to 60 minutes to Miraflores.
  • Daytime walking in Miraflores and Barranco is genuinely pleasant. Nights in Barranco are lively and fine with normal habits.

Section 05

Running

You are at sea level and mid taper toward the Julian Alps 50K, so treat Lima as an easy, aerobic block. Keep the legs turning, bank the ocean air, and save the intensity for the Alps.

The Daily Go To

Malecon, Miraflores

Flat · clifftop · car free path

The Malecon runs the length of the Miraflores cliffs above the Costa Verde, roughly 10 km of continuous car free path out and back if you connect through to San Isidro. Manicured parks, ocean on your right, soft and safe. This is your bread and butter run.

Long & Uninterrupted

Costa Verde south to Chorrillos

Flat · sea level · ~15 to 20 km

Drop to the Costa Verde bike path and run the coast south through Barranco toward Chorrillos and Herradura. Long, flat, and scenic, ideal for a taper long run. Go early before the beach traffic and haze build.

The Vert Option

Morro Solar & the bajadas

Rolling climbs · Chorrillos

If you want to keep some hill feel for the Alps, the cliff bajadas that drop to the coast are short, punchy climbs, and Morro Solar in Chorrillos gives a proper sustained ascent with big views over the bay. Add a few reps rather than a hard effort.

Conditions to know

  • September is Lima's gray season. Expect cool, humid, overcast mornings, which is honestly ideal running weather.
  • Sea level means no acclimation needed. Enjoy the easy breathing before the Alps altitude.
  • The Malecon and Costa Verde paths are busy and safe at dawn. Stick to them rather than street routes.
  • Hydration is simple at this effort. Carry a flask for anything over 90 minutes and refill at Malecon fountains.

Taper logic

  • Volume down, frequency steady. Short easy runs most days, one moderate long run on the weekend.
  • One light set of strides or short hill reps mid week keeps the legs sharp without cost.
  • Anniversary day gets a short shakeout only. The celebration is the priority.
  • Prioritize sleep and food. Lima makes the food part very easy.

Section 07

Weekend Side Trip

Paracas & the Ballestas

Desert meets ocean, about three and a half hours south, built around a flat coastal long run.

Getting there

Roughly 260 km down the Pan American Highway, three to three and a half hours by private driver. The road is good and fast. A driver beats a rental here, you want to sleep on the way back, not navigate. Leave Lima early Saturday to beat traffic and haze.

The weekend

Saturday morning, boat out to the Islas Ballestas for sea lions, Humboldt penguins and clouds of seabirds. Afternoon in the Paracas National Reserve where red sand cliffs drop into turquoise water. Stay clifftop at a Paracas resort with a pool and a spa for the anniversary glow. Sunday, the long run, then a slow lunch of scallops before the drive home.

The long run · Sunday

Distance
18 to 22 km easy
Terrain
Flat desert coast, hard packed
Elevation
Sea level, same as Lima
Climate
Warmer and sunnier than Lima, drier
Hydration
Carry all of it, no fountains
Timing
Dawn start, before the sun and wind

Run the malecon and reserve roads out and back along the bay. Empty, flat, and stunning at first light. Warmer and more exposed than Lima, so start early, carry every drop, and keep it fully aerobic. A clean taper long run with a view you will not forget.

Alternative if you want vines over ocean: Lunahuana, two to two and a half hours inland, for river rafting, pisco bodegas, and rolling valley roads to run.
