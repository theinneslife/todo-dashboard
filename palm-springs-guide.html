One Valley, Two Modes

The Shape of Twelve Days

November is the sweet spot in the desert. Highs in the mid twenties Celsius, pools still warm, nights cool enough to sleep with the doors open, and none of the January crowd. The two legs overlap on November 14 and 15, so treat this as one continuous stay with a change of gear in the middle.

Part 1 · Nov 10 to 15

The MNTN Meet

Work first. Palm Springs is a good offsite town precisely because it is small and everything is walking distance in the downtown grid. Book group dinners early, because the good rooms here seat forty people total and November is high season. Keep mornings for yourself, the desert is best before nine anyway.

Downtown Palm Springs is the walkable core. Everything else needs a car.
Part 2 · Nov 14 to 22

The actual holiday

Once the meet ends, the valley opens up. This is the leg for the aerial tramway, Joshua Tree, the long run, the mid century architecture, and the pool afternoons that are the entire point of Palm Springs.

Consider moving base for Part 2, from downtown to Old Las Palmas or La Quinta.
The Overlap

November 14 and 15

The two bookings overlap by two days. If both are held, use it: check into the second place on the 14th and let the work accommodation cover the last night of the meet. If not, one continuous booking is simpler and cheaper.

Worth confirming what is actually reserved for those two nights.

Section 1

Basics

Nine cities strung along one valley floor, thirty miles end to end, with a ten thousand foot mountain wall on the west side. You will drive every day.

Currency
US Dollar
Resort fees of 30 to 50 dollars a night are standard at the bigger properties. Tip 20 percent.
Mid Nov Weather
27 °C day
Dropping to about 12 °C overnight. Humidity in single digits. Essentially zero chance of rain.
Elevation
145 m
Valley floor. San Jacinto behind you rises to 3,302 m, which the tramway takes you most of the way up in ten minutes.
Getting around
Car, always
Uber works downtown but is thin elsewhere and expensive across the valley. Rent, and rent something with air conditioning that works.
Airport
PSP · 10 min
Palm Springs International is two miles from downtown and one of the most pleasant small airports in America. Outdoor gates.
The sun
Relentless
Even in November. Sunscreen, a hat and more water than feels necessary, especially on trails.

Practicalities

  • Very safe city. The main risks here are heat, dehydration and sunburn, in that order.
  • Trails close or become dangerous when hot. In November they are fine all day, but still start early.
  • Rattlesnakes are less active in November but not absent. Watch where you put your hands on rocky trails.
  • Restaurants close early by big city standards. Last seating is often 9pm and many kitchens shut Monday or Tuesday.
  • The Indian Canyons and Tahquitz Canyon are on Agua Caliente Band tribal land and charge an entrance fee. Cash or card at the gate.
  • Wind through the San Gorgonio Pass can be severe. If you are running near the windmills, check the forecast.

Section 4

Running

Two completely different worlds twenty minutes apart. Desert canyon singletrack on the valley floor, and alpine forest at 2,600 metres via a cable car.

The long run: Indian Canyons

The best trail running in the valley, on Agua Caliente tribal land at the south end of Palm Springs. Palm Canyon, Murray Canyon and Andreas Canyon connect into a network of real singletrack running through fan palm oases with actual water in them. The Palm Canyon Trail runs deep into the mountains and the Vandeventer and Fern Canyon loops let you build twenty five kilometres with fifteen hundred feet of climbing without repeating ground. Gate opens at 8am, entrance fee at the booth, and everything shuts at 5pm.

The special one: San Jacinto by tramway

The Palm Springs Aerial Tramway is the largest rotating aerial tramway in the world and lifts you from 2,643 feet to 8,516 feet in ten minutes, for around thirty dollars. At the top you step out into pine forest in Mount San Jacinto State Park at about 2,600 metres. Take the Round Valley and Wellman Divide trails, or push to San Jacinto Peak at 3,302 metres, a sixteen kilometre round trip with nine hundred metres of gain from the tram station. It is a genuine altitude session with a cable car for the descent, which is close to cheating and completely worth it.

Daily easy miles

The Tahquitz Creek and Whitewater channel paths give you flat, quiet, traffic free miles right out of downtown. For a scenic easy hour, run the streets of the Movie Colony and Old Las Palmas and look at the houses, which is the most Palm Springs thing possible. The Bump and Grind trail in Palm Desert is the local hill session, three hundred metres of climbing in three kilometres, busy but excellent.

Afternoons

Essential

Around thirty dollars, ten minutes, and a five thousand foot climb into a different climate. Go late afternoon, walk the loop at the top, and come down after sunset.

Mid century architecture tour

Citywide

Palm Springs has the densest collection of desert modernism anywhere. The Kaufmann House, Frey House II, the Tramway Gas Station. Do a guided tour with the Palm Springs Modernism organisation, or drive the self guided map through Twin Palms and Vista Las Palmas.

A three kilometre loop to a sixty foot waterfall inside the city limits, on tribal land, with a visitor centre and a ranger led option. The easiest genuinely beautiful hike here.

Section 6

Weekend Side Trip: Idyllwild

Built around the long run. An alpine village at 1,650 metres in the San Jacinto mountains, an hour from the desert floor and thirty degrees cooler in spirit.

Getting there

An hour by car from Palm Springs via Banning and Highway 243, or the more dramatic route up the 74 Palms to Pines Highway from Palm Desert. Both are winding mountain roads, both are spectacular, and neither has any public transport. Take the 74 up and the 243 down so you see both.

The long run: Devil's Slide to the PCT

From Humber Park above Idyllwild, the Devil's Slide Trail climbs four kilometres and four hundred metres to Saddle Junction, where it meets the Pacific Crest Trail. From there you can run north along the PCT towards Strawberry Junction and Wellman Divide, or south towards Tahquitz Peak and the fire lookout at 2,600 metres. A Tahquitz Peak loop is about eighteen kilometres with eight hundred metres of climbing, and a bigger PCT day gives you twenty eight kilometres. The forest is pine and cedar, the air is thin and cold, and it is one of the best trail systems in southern California.

Get the free San Jacinto Wilderness day use permit from the Idyllwild ranger station or online before you go, and check for early season snow above 2,400 metres. In mid November it is usually clear but not guaranteed.

The rest of the weekend

Stay at the Idyllwild Inn or a cabin in the pines. Eat at Gastrognome or Ferro. Coffee at Higher Grounds. The town has a golden retriever as its elected mayor, which tells you everything about the place. Come back down to the desert on Sunday afternoon and get in the pool.

Section 7

Shopping

Palm Springs is not a luxury capital, but it is one of the best places in America to buy mid century furniture and vintage, and there is a serious outlet mall on the way in.

Luxury

El Paseo

Palm Desert

The valley's actual luxury street, sometimes called the Rodeo Drive of the desert. Gucci, Saint Laurent, Tiffany, Louis Vuitton, plus galleries and good restaurants. Twenty minutes down valley.

Outlets

Desert Hills Premium Outlets

Cabazon

One of the largest luxury outlet centres in America, twenty minutes west on the I10. Prada, Gucci, Saint Laurent, Bottega. Go on a weekday morning.

Design

The Shops at Thirteen Forty Five

Palm Springs

A converted midcentury complex on North Palm Canyon housing several of the best design and vintage dealers under one roof. Start here.

Vintage Furniture

Uptown Design District

North Palm Canyon

Ten blocks of vintage and antique dealers. Modern Way, a la mod, Christopher Anthony. The best place in the country to buy authentic mid century pieces.

Menswear

Trina Turk and Mr Turk

North Palm Canyon

In an Albert Frey building, and the most Palm Springs clothing that exists. Bold prints, resort tailoring, and it works better than it should.

Vintage Clothing

Iconic Atomic and Dazzles

Palm Springs

Genuinely good vintage clothing and jewellery, well curated rather than thrifted. Worth an hour on a hot afternoon.

Books

Just Fabulous

Palm Springs

Independent bookshop and card store with a very strong design and architecture section. The right place for a Palm Springs modernism book.

Running

Fleet Feet Palm Desert

Palm Desert

The proper running shop in the valley, with local trail knowledge and gait analysis. Best source for trail intel on the Indian Canyons.
