The Main Event

Sky Trail 50K

Everything in this ten days points at one Friday morning. The I feel Slovenia Julian Alps Trail Run by UTMB runs September 18 to 20, 2026, and your race is the first one off the line.

The Numbers

55 km, 2,828 m

The Sky Trail 50K starts in the village of Zirovnica, climbs onto the ridge line above the Sava valley, traverses with the Julian Alps on one side and the Karawanks and Austria on the other, and drops into Kranjska Gora to finish. Roughly 9,300 feet of vertical gain over 34 miles.

Two waves on Friday Sep 18. First at 8:00am, second at 9:30am.
The Logistics Problem

Start and finish are 30 km apart

Zirovnica to Kranjska Gora is about 35 minutes by road. UTMB runs shuttles between the race village and the start, but on a point to point course with an 8am gun you want zero uncertainty. Book the official runner shuttle the day registration opens and treat a rental car as the backup, not the plan.

If you rent, park it outside the Ljubljana ring. City centre access is restricted and enforced.
Race Week Shape

Thursday and Friday are clear

You are not working Thursday or Friday of race week. Drive or train up to Kranjska Gora Wednesday evening, collect the bib Thursday, sleep at altitude near the finish, race Friday, and stay Friday night rather than driving down on cooked legs.

Two nights in Kranjska Gora beats a race morning transfer from Ljubljana.

Race day plan

4:45am
Up and eating. Three hours before the gun. Oats, banana, coffee, salt. Slovenian hotels will leave a breakfast box out if you ask the night before.
6:15am
Shuttle to Zirovnica. Allow 45 minutes plus buffer. Bring a throwaway layer, September mornings in that valley run near 8 degrees Celsius.
8:00am
Wave one. The first climb comes fast. Walk it early even when it feels absurd, the ridge rewards patience and punishes the first hour.
Mid race
The traverse. Exposed, technical in places, and the best running of the day. Eat on the flats because you will not want to on the climbs.
Finish
Kranjska Gora. Walk to the lake, get in the cold water for five minutes, then eat properly. Gostilna Pri Martinu in town does the right food for a wrecked body.

Safety and practicalities

  • Slovenia ranks among the safest countries in the world. Running alone before dawn in Ljubljana is genuinely fine.
  • Mountain weather changes fast in September. Carry a shell on every ridge run, no exceptions.
  • Tap water is excellent everywhere including from mountain fountains.
  • Vignette required for motorways if you rent. Buy it at the border or a petrol station before you drive.
  • Sunday closures are real outside Ljubljana. Shop Saturday.
  • Mountain rescue is world class but not free. Check that your travel insurance covers alpine trail running.
Section 2

The Shape of Ten Days

You are working 6pm to midnight most days, which makes Slovenia close to perfect. Mornings are yours and this country is a morning country.

Sep 12 to 16, Ljubljana

Settle in, run the Golovec and Roznik trails behind the city, taper properly, and eat your way through the best small food city in central Europe. Work the evenings. Do one recce day up to Bled or Vintgar to get the legs onto Alpine terrain.

Sep 16 to 19, Kranjska Gora

Move up Wednesday. Bib collection Thursday, easy shakeout on the Vrsic road, race Friday, stay Friday night. Saturday is for the lake, the sauna and doing nothing.

Sep 19 to 21, the Soca or Bled

Recovery days in the most beautiful part of the country. Bovec and the Soca valley if you want the river, Bled if you want comfort and a short drive back to the airport.

Section 5

Running

Ljubljana is a genuinely great running city and the Alps are 40 minutes away. Taper here, sharpen there.

Daily from Ljubljana

The Path of Remembrance and Comradeship is a 33 kilometre gravel and dirt loop that circles the entire city on the line of the old occupation fence. Flat, tree lined, soft underfoot and marked the whole way. It is the single best urban run in central Europe and you can join it from anywhere. For hills, Roznik is 15 minutes from the centre with 200 metres of gain on forest trail, and Golovec on the south side gives you a proper 400 metre climb on singletrack.

Roznik
Easy hills, forest

Race specific sharpening, Sep 14 to 16

The 50K is a ridge race with sustained climbing, so do not spend taper week on flat gravel. Drive to Bled on the Monday or Tuesday and run the Ojstrica and Mala Osojnica loop, short but steep with the classic lake view at the top. Then take the Vintgar Gorge boardwalk out and the forest trail back for an easy hour. That is enough Alpine specificity without digging a hole.

Recovery, Sep 19 to 21

Kranjska Gora to Jasna lake and back is four flat kilometres on a paved path, which is all your legs should do on Saturday. Sunday, if the body allows, the Soca Trail from the source down through Bovec is soft, riverside and one of the most beautiful easy runs in Europe.

Afternoons

One good thing per afternoon, scaled from light in taper week to bigger once the race is done.

Light

Ride up, walk the ramparts, climb the viewing tower. Ninety minutes, no legs required, and the best orientation to the city.

Central Market and Plecnik colonnade

Vodnikov trg

Joze Plecnik designed most of what makes Ljubljana beautiful. Walk the covered market colonnade, buy cheese and figs, then see the Triple Bridge and the National Library.

Signature Local

Beekeeping and honey at Radovljica

Radovljica

Slovenia is the beekeeping capital of Europe, with its own native bee and painted hive panels. The Museum of Apiculture plus a working apiary visit is the most Slovenian afternoon available.

Memorable

Vintgar Gorge boardwalk

Near Bled

A kilometre and a half of wooden walkway pinned to a limestone gorge above turquoise water. Timed entry, book online, go at 8am to have it nearly alone.

Memorable

Postojna Cave and Predjama Castle

Postojna

An electric train into 24 kilometres of karst cave, then a castle built into a cliff mouth. Forty five minutes from Ljubljana and genuinely astonishing.

Post Race

Lake Bled island and the cream cake

Bled

Row a pletna boat to the island, ring the wishing bell, then eat kremsnita on a terrace. Cliche and completely worth it.

Post Race

Soca river, Bovec

Bovec

The most unreasonably coloured river in Europe. Rafting and canyoning still run in September, or just walk the Soca Trail and swim if you are brave.

Vipava

An hour west, Slovenia's most exciting wine region. Natural and orange wines at Batic and Burja, and lunch at Majerija. Hire a driver.

Section 7

The Alps: Kranjska Gora and the Soca

Your side trip is not optional, it is the race. Here is how to make the days around it worth the flight.

Getting there

Ljubljana to Kranjska Gora is 85 kilometres and about an hour by car on the A2 motorway. Buy the vignette before you drive. Public transport works but takes over two hours with a change at Jesenice, so for race logistics a car is worth the friction. Park it in Kranjska Gora and leave it there, everything in the village is walkable.

Where to stay in Kranjska Gora

Hotel Kotnik is the small, characterful choice right in the village centre with a very good restaurant attached. Ramada Resort is the practical one with a pool and spa for the day after. Book either the moment you have the bib, the whole valley fills for UTMB week.

The Vrsic pass, once you can drive again

Fifty hairpins over the highest mountain pass in Slovenia, built by prisoners of war in 1915, dropping down into the Trenta valley and on to Bovec. It is one of the great drives in Europe and it is also the road you will look at on Thursday and correctly decide not to run.

The two genuine Slovenian luxury crafts. Rogaska hand blown crystal and hand made bobbin lace from Idrija, both with centuries behind them.

Slovenian forest honey, dark green Styrian pumpkin seed oil, and hand harvested Piran sea salt. The three things worth carrying home.
