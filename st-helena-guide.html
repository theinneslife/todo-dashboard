One Town, Two Very Different Weeks

The Shape of Nine Days

St Helena in the second half of October is the best week of the Napa year. Harvest is finishing, the vines turn gold and red, the crowds have gone home, and the valley smells like fermenting fruit from one end to the other. You get five quiet days of it and then three days of a wedding.

Part 1 · Oct 17 to 22

The training week

Airbnb, a rental bike from St Helena Cyclery on Main Street, and the best cycling and running roads in California outside the Sierra. This is the block to get real volume in before three days of eating and toasting. Silverado Trail at 7am with the fog burning off the vines is worth the trip on its own.

Bike drops back at the Cyclery Saturday afternoon before the wedding. Set a reminder.
Part 2 · Oct 22 to 25

The wedding

Hotel booked, driving between the two, and a different set of rules. Group meals, a schedule you do not control, and a lot of very good wine. Plan one morning run and one recovery activity per day and let everything else be the wedding.

Napa wedding weekends usually run Friday welcome, Saturday ceremony, Sunday brunch.
Logistics

The car, and the two airports

Rent in SFO on arrival, return it to Santa Rosa STS at the end. That is an unusual one way but it saves the drive back down to the peninsula, and STS is a twenty five minute drive from St Helena instead of ninety. Confirm the one way drop fee when you book.

SFO to St Helena is about 90 minutes with no traffic and two hours with.

Section 1

The Wedding Weekend

A Napa wedding has its own physics. Here is how to be the guest who enjoys all of it and still runs on Sunday.

Where the group stays

If people are still booking, the useful map is this. Meadowood and Auberge du Soleil are the two grand resort options and both are walking distance from nothing, so they need drivers. Harvest Inn and Las Alcobas sit at the edge of St Helena and are the best compromise between comfort and being able to walk to Main Street. Calistoga is fifteen minutes north, noticeably cheaper, and has the hot springs. For a block of guests, the Francis House and Solage in Calistoga or a large rental in the hills above Angwin are the ones that keep everyone together.

Where a group eats

Wedding weekends need places that take twelve without an argument. Farmstead at Long Meadow Ranch has the barn, the lawn and the capacity, and it is genuinely good. Goose and Gander has the basement bar and a patio that absorbs a crowd. Gott's Roadside is the correct lunch for a large group who have all been drinking. Brasswood and Charlie Palmer Steak in Napa both do private rooms. For a rehearsal dinner scale room, Tre Posti in St Helena is purpose built for exactly this.

The wedding day itself

October ceremonies in Napa usually start between 4:30 and 5:30 to catch the light, which means a long open bar afternoon before anyone eats. Run at seven, eat a real breakfast, drink water on a schedule rather than by thirst, and eat something substantial at three even if it feels like it will spoil dinner. Nights in the valley drop to nine or ten degrees Celsius after dark, so anything outdoors will be genuinely cold by nine. Bring a layer and tell Brooke to as well.

Recovery runs across the wedding days

Keep them short, flat and early. The Napa Valley Vine Trail from St Helena south is paved, flat and quiet at 7am. The loop around Lake Hennessey east of Rutherford is eight kilometres of rolling road with almost no traffic. Both let you be back before anybody else is awake, which is the whole trick to a wedding weekend.

Section 2

Basics

Small town, one main street, and a valley that runs thirty miles from Napa to Calistoga. Everything is on Highway 29 or Silverado Trail.

Currency
US Dollar
Tasting fees run 75 to 350 dollars a head and are often waived with a purchase. Tip tasting room staff 20 dollars a person.
Late Oct Weather
24 °C day
Dropping to about 8 °C at night. Morning fog until nine, brilliant afternoons. First rain of the season is possible.
Elevation
70 m
Valley floor. The mountain roads climb to 700 m on both sides, which is where the good riding is.
Getting around
Car essential
Uber and Lyft exist but are thin and slow, especially at night. For wine days book a driver, not a rideshare.
Harvest
Crush, ending
Late October is the tail of harvest. Cabernet is coming in, the wineries smell extraordinary, and everyone is exhausted and happy.
Airports
SFO in, STS out
SFO is 90 minutes. Santa Rosa STS is 25 minutes and flies Alaska. Oakland is a good third option.

Practicalities

  • Almost every winery in Napa is appointment only by law. Turning up unannounced does not work. Book two weeks ahead minimum.
  • Two tastings a day is the maximum if you want to remember either. Three is a mistake and four is a lost day.
  • October is fire season's tail. Check Cal Fire and air quality if there is any smoke, and have a flexible plan.
  • Highway 29 through St Helena backs up badly from 4pm. Use Silverado Trail instead, it is faster and prettier.
  • Ship wine home rather than carrying it. Every tasting room does it and it costs less than a checked bag.
  • Deer and cyclists on Silverado Trail at dawn. Drive slowly, and if you are riding, use lights.

Wineries

Appointment only, two a day maximum, and all of these are inside fifteen minutes of St Helena. Book before you fly.

The Icon

Schramsberg

Calistoga

Sparkling wine aged in hand dug caves from the 1860s, the bottles stacked to the ceiling by candlelight. The best tour in Napa and the least like everything else.

The Icon

Inglenook

Rutherford

Francis Ford Coppola's restored nineteenth century chateau, and one of the great historic estates of California. The Heritage tasting is worth the money.

Cult Cabernet

Corison

St Helena

Cathy Corison has been making the most elegant, least overblown Cabernet in Napa for forty years, in a barn on Highway 29. The single best tasting in the valley.

Cult Cabernet

Spottswoode

St Helena

Organic, biodynamic, walking distance from town, and one of the most respected estates in California. Small groups only, so book early.

Mountain

Cade or Ovid

Howell Mountain / Pritchard Hill

Go up. The views from the eastern hills over the whole valley are the reason to leave the floor, and mountain fruit is a completely different wine.

Architecture

Hall St Helena and Quixote

St Helena / Stags Leap

Hall for the Bunny Foo Foo sculpture and the modern estate. Quixote for the Hundertwasser building, which looks like a mushroom and has no straight lines.

Group Friendly

Charles Krug and Beringer

St Helena

The two oldest wineries in Napa, both on the edge of St Helena, both able to take a wedding sized group with notice. Beringer's Rhine House is a National Historic Landmark.

Something Different

Round Pond olive mill

Rutherford

Olive oil rather than wine, milled on site, with a tasting that is as serious as any wine flight and half the alcohol. The right thing to do on the morning of the wedding.

Section 6

Running and Riding

This is one of the great endurance training valleys in America, which is why every pro cyclist in northern California trains here. Five days of it before the wedding is a gift.

The long run: Oat Hill Mine Trail, Calistoga

The best trail run in Napa County. An old wagon road from 1893 climbing out of Calistoga up the side of the valley, with wheel ruts still cut into the rock. Roughly eight kilometres of sustained climbing to the Palisades junction and 750 metres of gain, then you can extend along the Palisades Trail towards Table Rock for a genuinely big day. Run it as an out and back for twenty to twenty six kilometres with two thousand feet of climbing, and finish in the hot springs at Indian Springs.

The other trail options

Bothe Napa Valley State Park is five minutes from St Helena with the Ritchey Canyon and Coyote Peak loop, about ten kilometres on shaded redwood and fir singletrack, and it is the best easy trail run in the valley. Skyline Wilderness Park in Napa has a bigger network if you want thirty kilometres. Moore Creek Park east of the valley is the quietest, hardest and least known.

The road: Silverado Trail and the mountain crossings

With a bike from St Helena Cyclery, the classic ride is Silverado Trail north to Calistoga and back, about fifty kilometres of rolling two lane road with a shoulder and vineyards the whole way. For climbing, the Oakville Grade over to Sonoma is four kilometres at nine percent and one of the hardest short climbs in the region. Mount Veeder and Howell Mountain both give you serious sustained work. The Napa Valley Vine Trail is the paved, traffic free option for easy spinning and for riding with Brooke.

Bike logistics

St Helena Cyclery is on Main Street, rents proper road bikes, and knows every route in the valley. Collect on arrival, and remember it goes back Saturday afternoon before the wedding, which means your last real ride is Friday morning.

Section 7

Afternoons

Things to do in the valley that are not drinking, which matters over nine days.

Light

Main Street, St Helena

St Helena

Six blocks of nineteenth century stone buildings, good shops and the best small town main street in California. An hour, easily.

Light

Culinary Institute at Greystone

St Helena

The CIA's west coast campus in an 1889 stone winery. Cooking demonstrations, a restaurant, and one of the great buildings in the valley.

Signature Local

Calistoga mud bath

Calistoga

Volcanic ash, peat and naturally heated mineral water. Indian Springs has been doing it since 1862 and it is the correct thing to do the day after a big run.

Memorable

Hot air balloon at dawn

Yountville

Lifting off at sunrise over the vineyards in harvest colour. Expensive, over by nine, and the single best photograph of the trip.

Memorable

Old Faithful of California and the Petrified Forest

Calistoga

Two genuinely odd roadside attractions that are much better than they sound. A geyser that erupts every half hour and a redwood forest turned to stone by Mount St Helena.

Culture

Robert Louis Stevenson State Park

Mount St Helena

Stevenson honeymooned here in 1880. The summit hike is sixteen kilometres return with six hundred metres of gain and views to the Pacific and the Sierra on a clear day.

Day Out

Healdsburg and the Russian River

45 min west

Over the hill into Sonoma. Prettier town square, better Pinot Noir, less formality. Lunch at Valette or SingleThread if you plan far enough ahead.

Day Out

The Sonoma Coast

90 min west

Bodega Bay and the Sonoma Coast State Park. Cold, wild, empty, and the perfect antidote to a week of vineyards. Oysters at The Marshall Store on the way.

Section 8

Shopping

There is no luxury mall here and pretending otherwise wastes a morning. What St Helena has is an unusually good main street and a few genuinely special shops.

The Landmark

Woodhouse Chocolate

Main St, St Helena

In a former bank building fitted out like a Paris jewellery salon. Some of the best chocolate made in America and the most giftable thing in the valley.

Home

Erin Martin Design

St Helena

Enormous, theatrical design store full of objects you will not see anywhere else. Worth walking through even if you buy nothing.

Fashion

Pearl Wonderful Clothing and Elyse Walker

Napa / St Helena

The two best places in the county to buy actual clothes. Elyse Walker's Napa store carries the luxury edit you would find in Los Angeles.

Kitchen

Shackford's Kitchen Store

Napa

An unglamorous, extraordinary cookware shop in downtown Napa. Chefs from across the valley shop here. Cash and check only, which tells you everything.

Books

Napa Bookmine and Main Street Books

Napa / St Helena

Two good independent bookshops, and Main Street Books in St Helena is a tiny, perfectly curated used shop.

Cycling

St Helena Cyclery

Main St, St Helena

Your bike source and the best local route intelligence in the valley. Ask them where to ride and then ride exactly that.

The main reformer option in the upper valley, small classes, drop in friendly. Book by phone, they are not app driven.

Mat based studios in town, walk in friendly, and the practical choice on a wedding morning.

The full service club in the county, with pools, courts and a proper weight room. Day passes available to visitors.

Recovery

Indian Springs, Calistoga

Calistoga

Volcanic mud bath, mineral pool at 40 degrees, and the oldest continuously operating pool in California. The single best recovery in the valley.

Recovery

St Helena / Rutherford

The two resort spas, both open to non guests for treatments. Book the day after the long run, or the morning after the wedding.

Groceries

For the Airbnb week. St Helena punches far above its size here, because the whole valley cooks.

The Local Whole Foods

Sunshine Foods

Main St, St Helena

The independent grocery that serves the entire upper valley, with a deli, a butcher and a wine section better than most specialist shops. This is your store.

Gourmet

Oakville Grocery

Oakville

Open since 1881 and the original Napa gourmet store. Sandwiches, cheese and picnic supplies. The right stop before a vineyard picnic.

Market Hall

Oxbow Public Market

Napa

Twenty five minutes south. Hog Island oysters, Ritual coffee, a butcher, a fishmonger and the Model Bakery. Worth a dedicated trip.

Farmers Market

St Helena Farmers Market

Crane Park · Friday mornings

May through October, so late October is the tail of the season. Go on the Friday of Part 1 for the last of the summer produce.

Full Range

Cal Mart, Calistoga

Calistoga

The other good independent grocer in the upper valley, with a very good deli counter. Useful if you are staying up valley.

Farm Stand

St Helena

Farm eggs, produce and estate olive oil. Napa Valley Olive Oil Manufacturing on Charter Oak Avenue has been running since 1931 and is a genuine institution.
